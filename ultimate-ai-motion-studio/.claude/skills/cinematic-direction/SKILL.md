# Cinematic Direction
- Composition: rule of thirds, leading lines, foreground/mid/background layers.
- Moves: dolly-in for focus, orbit for scale, crane/rise for reveal, tracking for travel. One dominant move per scene.
- Transitions: connect scenes with a camera travel (fly-through, match-position), not cross-dissolves. Never reuse the same transition twice in a row.
- Lighting: key + rim + low ambient; warm/cool contrast. Fog for atmosphere.
- Depth of field and volumetrics: only if performance allows; otherwise fake with fog and scale.
- Timing: hold 0.5-1s before a reveal, land key beats on voiceover stress words.
- Original work only; do not recreate existing film shots.
